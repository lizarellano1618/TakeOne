# Home Alone (1990) 

## Overview 

Written by John Hughes and directed by Chris Columbus, *Home Alone* is one of the highest-grossing holiday films of all time. The narrative centers on eight year old Kevin McCallister, who is accidentally left behind when his massive family flies to Paris for Christmas vacation. While initially enjoying his absolute freedom, Kevin quickly discovers his home is being targeted by the "Wet Bandits," a pair of notorious neighborhood burglars. 
## Defensive Strategy 

Instead of hiding, Kevin turns his house into a defensive fortress using brilliant, painful traps. This specific concept of resourceful kids defending their home turf heavily influenced subsequent action movies, including the brotherly defenses in [[3-ninjas]].  

### Iconic Elements 

* **The Traps:** Micro-Machines on the floor, swinging paint cans, and red-hot doorknobs. 

* **The Message:** The underlying story emphasizes the deep importance of family cohesion, a theme shared by contemporary sports dramas like [[the-mighty-ducks]].  

> "I made my family disappear." – Kevin

Kevin's journey from a frightened child to a confident protector makes this an immortal classic of youth cinema.
